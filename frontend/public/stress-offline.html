How did the committee behave in two crises?

All 356 scheduled decisions completed. COVID and 2022 rate hikes, US and India. These are retrospective simulations, not verified real trades or guaranteed future performance.

This report contains actual recorded outputs, not a hypothetical sales demo. It tests the current rules-based committee; Gemini was not active. No claim is made that the system predicted either crisis in advance.

If you started with $10,000

Completed US COVID test, February–June 2020. Four equal starting portions: SPY, AAPL, JPM and XOM. The committee leaves unused money in cash.

Buy-and-hold invests all the money. A smaller loss with little exposure is not proof of good predictions. “Always buy, same sizing” uses the committee’s entry dates, sizes and stop distances, but always bets on a rise.

How this was tested

  1. Freeze daily prices from Yahoo Finance and check dates, missing values and price bounds.
  2. Train the separate price models only through December 2019 for COVID, and December 2021 for the 2022 test.
  3. At each date, run production GARCH, market-regime, Isolation Forest and LSTM models on past prices only.
  4. Run actual FinBERT on already-published, curated summaries of official Fed/WHO announcements. Missing news stays unavailable.
  5. Run the original and revised committee on identical evidence, then reveal the next five sessions only to the trade evaluator.
  6. Enter at the next open. Use the stated fixed stop, worse opening-gap fills, dividends, 0.30% round-trip costs and 3% annual short-borrow assumption.

What remains unproven

  • Historical data and current rules do not prove future performance. The instruments were selected from surviving large caps.
  • These are hypothetical fractional positions, not broker executions. Short availability, Indian overnight-short arrangements, whole-share/lot sizes, taxes, cash interest and funding are not established.
  • News coverage is narrow and curated. FinBERT’s wording score is not event truth or an economic forecast. Gemini quality is untested.
  • The price predictor is a separate panel: its probabilities do not drive the production CRO decision.
  • Closing-balance drawdown can miss worse intraday losses. Yahoo prices may have been revised and use a split-adjusted basis.
  • The calibration state is a frozen starting prior. Ratings are not measured probabilities of profit.
